# Dirt material

Dirt by Charlotte Baglioni, Poly Haven: https://polyhaven.com/a/dirt
CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

1K JPEG diffuse, OpenGL normal, and roughness maps downloaded from the asset's
API manifest at https://api.polyhaven.com/files/dirt and MD5-verified on download.
The scanned maps are combined with procedural compaction/rut variation.
